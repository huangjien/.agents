# Template: Explanation

> **Use when:** the user asks "what is X?" or "explain how Y works".
> This template is a starting structure, not a fixed shape — adapt to the
> question.

---

## TL;DR

One-sentence answer to the question. The reader should be able to stop here
and have the gist.

## What it is

Two or three sentences. Define the term or system in plain language.
Name the closest familiar analog if one exists ("X is like Y, but…").

## How it works

A short narrative (3–6 sentences) explaining the mechanism. Where it
helps, use a numbered list; where it hurts, use a diagram.

## Example

Concrete, runnable if possible. One short example beats three paragraphs
of abstract description.

## When to use it vs. when not to

Two bullets. Plain-language criteria for whether to reach for this
thing in a given situation.

## Common pitfalls

One or two bullets. The mistake people make most often.

## Where to read more

One or two links with one-line descriptions of what's at each. Pick the
most authoritative first.
